# AIT530 Assignments

Coursework for AIT 530 (Grand Canyon University), by Yashu Lanki.
Course Title - Statistics & Data Analysis for AI

## Projects

- [`Week 01 - Probability Distributions/`](./Week%2001%20-%20Probability%20Distributions) — *Probability Distributions and the Central Limit Theorem with Bike Sharing Data*. Calculates and visualizes descriptive statistics for the UCI Bike Sharing dataset, matches each variable to a discrete or continuous distribution, computes binomial, Poisson, and normal probabilities, and demonstrates the central limit theorem with sampling distributions and confidence intervals.
- [`Week 02 - Statistical Inference/`](./Week%2002%20-%20Statistical%20Inference) — *Estimating Population Parameters with Confidence Intervals: Bank Marketing Data*. Calculates point estimates and 95% confidence intervals for the mean client age and the proportion who subscribe in the UCI Bank Marketing dataset, then repeats the sampling across sample sizes to show how sample size and variability affect the precision and accuracy of the estimates.
